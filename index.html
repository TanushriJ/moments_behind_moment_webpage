How the privacy shield works in practice +

Temporary disguises, not permanent encryption: To give you helpful answers, the assistant needs to understand spending patterns. We temporarily disguise your details by masking account digits, scaling amounts, and replacing sensitive merchant names with aliases. Once the answer returns, it translates back on your computer so you see your real numbers.

What reaches the AI: The AI only receives the filtered, masked snippet needed to answer your specific question. Broad categories remain intact so insights make sense, but your identity and full account details are never shared.

About this demonstration: All transactions shown on this page are fictional examples with a fixed 2× scale. Exploring this site does not upload or collect any personal data.

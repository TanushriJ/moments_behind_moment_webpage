01 / MASK

Reduce identifying detail.

Mask account identifiers and replace detected emails, phone numbers, and card numbers with tags.

02 / TRANSFORM

Keep patterns useful.

For chat data, sensitive merchants become aliases and monetary values are scaled by a session factor.

03 / RESTORE

Make the answer yours.

Map aliases and scaled figures back to readable names and original amounts in the response you see.

Understand the scope of the privacy layer +

These are reversible transformations (pseudonymization), rather than complete anonymity or encryption. MBM retains the mapping needed to restore replies. Patterns and categories can still reveal context.

Merchant aliasing and amount scaling apply to chat data. Non-sensitive merchant names may remain visible depending on category settings, and other AI features can use different inputs. Account masking retains the last four characters. Cloud AI may receive filtered data, depending on the deployment and provider settings.

This demonstration uses fictional data and a fixed 2× scale. No personal data is uploaded by these examples.
